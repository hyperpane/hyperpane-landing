Windows

Every tool is a window. Add the ones you need.

Three come built in. The rest are a click away in Plugins, and each opens as one more window on your canvas.

Built in

Terminal

Your shell, on any of your machines, that keeps running when you close the app.

Editor

Any file, with highlighting, find and replace and multiple cursors.

Explorer

Your folders as a list: sort, search, drag to move, open in the Editor.

Your own

Missing a tool? Make it a window like any other.

Always on

Close the app. Your work keeps running.

Terminals, agents and long tasks don’t stop when you close hyperpane. Open it again and everything is where you left it.

  • Quitting only detaches

    Builds, tests and agents carry on without you.

  • Exactly where you left it

    Reopen the project: same windows, same places, with everything they printed meanwhile.

  • No babysitting

    Start a build or an agent, close the lid, and come back to the result.
