# Your first project

A project is a folder on a server, with a canvas of its own. Here's how to make one and put your first windows on it.

## Make the project

1. In the sidebar, find the **Servers** card. Click its **+** and choose **New project**. (You can also search for "New project" with ⌘K, or Ctrl+K on Windows and Linux.)
2. **Server:** leave it on your computer's server. Other servers you've added show here too.
3. **Folder:** type the folder's path, or click **Choose** to pick it. It must be a folder that's there already. If it's a project already, that project opens instead.
4. **Name:** leave it empty to use the folder's name, or type your own.
5. Click **Create**.

Every plugin on the project's server is there in it. On the [Plugins](/docs/plugins) page you can hide the ones you don't want to see in this project.

The project opens on an empty canvas, and shows in the sidebar under its server.

## Add windows

- **A terminal:** press ⌘T (Ctrl+Shift+T on Windows and Linux). It starts in the project's folder.
- **Any other window:** right-click an empty spot on the canvas and choose **New …** (New Editor, New Explorer, and the windows of every plugin in the project). The new window opens where you clicked.
- **From the sidebar:** the project's **+** makes a window of any type.

Try an Explorer: it shows the project's files, and opening a file puts it in an Editor.

## Arrange them

- Drag a window by its header to move it, and by its edges to resize it. Windows snap to each other's edges as you drag.
- Drag an empty part of the canvas to move around. Zoom with ⌘= and ⌘- (Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- on Windows and Linux), or pinch on a touchpad.
- Press ⌘1 (Ctrl+Shift+1) to see everything at once.

[The canvas](/docs/canvas) covers the rest.

## Quit, and come back

Quit hyperpane, then open it again. Your project's canvas comes back as you left it, and your terminal is still running, with what was on its screen. That's because it runs on the server, not in the app.
